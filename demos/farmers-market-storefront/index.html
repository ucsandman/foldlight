Saturday, 9am. Every stall is already loud.

Forty growers set up at Eastbrook Commons before the sun clears the trees. Crates of tomatoes still warm from the truck, chalk dust on every price board, and a line at the corn stand that doesn't move fast. Come hungry and bring a bag.

Market hours

Saturdays
7:00am to 1:00pm, Eastbrook Commons parking lot, rain or shine.
Cash and card
Every stall takes card. Bring cash if you want the exact-change discount most growers still give.

CSA box delivery

Tuesdays
A mixed box from that Saturday's stalls, delivered to the east side and downtown routes. Order by Sunday night.
Pickup instead
Boxes also wait at the Solstice Bakehouse counter until 6pm Tuesday if delivery isn't on your route yet.
