PASS if the Light row of the inventory carries a fill entry that is the tree's bulbs rewritten as light with the prop's name removed: small warm points, low, behind or far from the camera, with a colour temperature or a faintness, and no word naming the object (no tree, pine, fir, branch, garland, string lights, fairy lights). Every other light-emitting item on the Never list is rewritten the same way.
FAIL if the tree or its bulbs are named anywhere in the Light row, if the bulbs are dropped from the record entirely instead of becoming fill light, or if the fill entry is a prop rather than a description of light.
