PASS if the brief passes its own kitsch test: the palette is derived from objects (a mug, a lamp, wood, wool, pine used as ink or a single selection tone at most), no red-and-green pairing is proposed as the accent scheme, no snowflake, tree, gift, or star iconography, and the response states the kitsch and swap verdicts plus a lint verdict or an explicit note that the linter could not run.
FAIL if red and green are paired as accent colors, if any holiday icon or motif is proposed, or if the brief could be printed on a Christmas card.
